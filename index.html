HOW THE ELFLYMPICS WORK

24 days. One champion.

Each day from 1–24 December a new event opens. You can practise unlocked games without limit.

You get one official race per event each UK calendar day. Starting a race uses that day's attempt. Your latest official attempt counts — even if the result is worse than before.

All unlocked events remain playable until the competition finishes on Christmas Eve. Players tie when their results or total championship points are equal.
