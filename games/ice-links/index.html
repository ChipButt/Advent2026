EVENT RULES

Link the checkpoints.

  1. Join islands in a straight horizontal or vertical line, to the next island in that direction.
  2. An island's number tells you exactly how many bridges touch it.
  3. You may build one or two bridges between the same pair of islands. Tap the pair again to cycle through one, two and none.
  4. Bridges never cross or pass through an island.
  5. To win, all the islands must be connected in one network, with every count satisfied.

Tap two checkpoints, drag from one to another, or tap a track to change it. Green checkpoints are complete, red means too many tracks. Every checkpoint must connect. Solve five consecutive puzzles to finish; the next board appears automatically. There are no separate medals.
